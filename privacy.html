Privacy Policy - Auto Clipper Personal

Last updated: October 2026

Auto Clipper Personal ("the app") is a personal tool used only by its owner.

What data the app accesses

  • YouTube (Google): when the owner connects a YouTube channel, the app receives an OAuth token from Google with permission to upload videos (youtube.upload) and to read the channel name (youtube.readonly). It is used only to upload videos the owner chooses to the owner's own channel.
  • TikTok: an access token and basic profile information (display name) through TikTok Login Kit, used to upload videos the owner chooses to the owner's TikTok account.
  • Instagram & Facebook: a Page access token, used to publish videos the owner chooses to the owner's own Page and Instagram account.

How data is used and stored

  • Tokens and settings are stored only on the owner's own computer. The app has no server.
  • The app does not sell, transfer or share any data with third parties, and does not use it for advertising.
  • The app does not collect data from any other users.
  • The app's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Deleting data

The owner can disconnect any account inside the app at any time, which deletes the stored token. Access can also be revoked at myaccount.google.com/permissions (Google), in TikTok under Settings and privacy > Security > Manage app permissions, and in Facebook under Settings > Apps and Websites.
